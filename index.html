Pigment

Color Mix Puzzle for iPhone and iPad

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas — write to artemh1994@gmail.com. I read every message and usually answer within two days.

How to play

A color appears. Mix it. On some levels you tap the paint jars to add drops. On others the whole screen is the mixing surface: slide left and right for the mix, up and down for how light it is. When the color matches, it dissolves into the background.

Riddles are a separate mode. Each one names an action — stir, shake, flip — and some use the camera, microphone or your rough location. Every riddle asks for permission first and gives up its answer after a while if you are stuck.
